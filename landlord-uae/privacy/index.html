LANDLORD UAE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Mobile app for iOS and Android

Overview

Landlord UAE (“the App”) is a mobile application for independently managing long-term residential rental properties. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. The App is provided by the independent developer identified on its official app-store listing. Effective date: 3 November 2026.

Information you enter

You may enter property addresses and descriptions, tenancy and lease details, tenant names and contact information, rent schedules, payment and post-dated cheque records, expense records, maintenance issues, inspection photographs, documents, reminders and notes. These records can contain personal information about you and other people. You decide what to enter and are responsible for having an appropriate basis to keep and use other people's information.

Where rental records are stored

The App is designed to keep rental portfolio records on your device rather than upload them to a developer-operated portfolio database. It does not require a Landlord UAE cloud account. Ordinary entry, viewing and editing of rental records do not require the developer to receive your tenant details, inspection photos or tenancy documents. Some subscription and platform services do require network connections and process limited information as described below. Your device operating system may independently include application data in device-level backups according to your settings.

Backups, exports and sharing

If you choose to create a backup, the App produces an encrypted backup file that you can save or transfer using your device's available sharing and storage options. You control where the file goes and must safeguard its password or recovery key. If you save it to a cloud drive, messaging service or another device, that third party's terms and privacy practices apply. The developer does not automatically receive your backup file and cannot recover a lost recovery key. Likewise, files or reports you explicitly share are sent using your chosen destination.

Subscriptions and purchases

The App offers optional paid subscription access after an initial editing period. Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple's in-app purchase system (on iOS) processes payments. RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restore entitlements and manage subscription status. For these purposes, the platform and RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, transaction identifiers or tokens, an app-specific subscriber identifier and limited device or technical information. RevenueCat may process this information outside the United Arab Emirates. The developer does not receive full payment-card numbers. See Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Permissions and notifications

With your action or permission, the App may access selected photos or files to attach supporting evidence or restore backups, and may schedule device notifications for payment dates, renewals and other reminders. You can manage relevant permissions in your device settings. Selected content is used for the requested feature; it is not uploaded to the developer's servers as part of normal rental record keeping.

Advertising, analytics and support

The App does not display third-party advertising and is not designed to track you across other companies' apps for advertising. The developer does not use the contents of your rental portfolio for marketing analytics. Google Play, Apple and subscription-service providers may supply limited transaction or operational information associated with their own services. If you contact support, the developer receives whatever contact details, diagnostic descriptions or attachments you voluntarily send. Please avoid sending real tenant records unless strictly necessary.

Security

Rental records are protected by your device's security controls and any App Lock features that you enable. User-created export backups are encrypted. Access to external subscription services uses secured connections provided by those services. No device, backup process or transmission method is completely risk-free. Keep your device updated, restrict access and securely store recovery information.

Retention, access and deletion

Rental records ordinarily remain on your device until you edit or remove them or remove the App's local data. Uninstalling the App generally deletes its local data, subject to device or operating-system backup and restore behavior. Backup files, exported reports and copies that you shared or stored elsewhere must be deleted separately by you. The developer cannot remotely inspect, retrieve, correct or erase records that exist solely on your device. Subscription transaction records may be retained by Google, Apple and RevenueCat under their respective policies and legal obligations. You can contact the developer to request deletion of eligible data held through support or subscription services; verification of the subscription identifier may be required. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.

Children and international processing

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over, not for children. Rental portfolio contents are not intentionally sent by the App to an international developer-hosted database. Limited purchase and technical data processed by Google, Apple or RevenueCat may be handled in other countries under their privacy practices.

Your privacy questions

For questions, access requests or deletion requests concerning data received by the developer or subscription service, contact the developer at stanislav.ismagilov@proton.me. When you make a request, include sufficient details to identify the relevant subscription or support correspondence, but do not send tenant records or passwords. For information kept only on your device, use the App's editing or deletion functions and manage any separately exported files yourself.

Changes

This policy may be updated when App functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with its effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
